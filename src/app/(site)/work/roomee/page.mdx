## The problem

Finding a room in Morocco as a student runs on WhatsApp groups, Facebook posts, and word of mouth. Listings are duplicated across a dozen places, most are stale, and the ones that are real are often priced for people with a salary. The search that matters is not "show me apartments in this city", it is "show me the places I can actually afford, near where I study, that are still available".

## What I built

I lead engineering on Roomee. Three pieces carry most of the weight:

**Listing ingestion.** Supply arrives from scattered, inconsistent sources. Ingestion normalizes it into one shape and collapses duplicates, so a single flat posted in four places appears once.

**Search.** Filtering on price, location, and availability, tuned for the query a renter actually has rather than the fields a database happens to store.

**Matching.** Pairing renters with places within their budget and their commute, which is the part that turns a listing directory into something worth coming back to.
