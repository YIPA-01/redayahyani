## The problem

Parole is a schedule of obligations with real consequences for missing one: appointments, check-ins, conditions that vary per person. The people managing that schedule are the least well served by a normal web app. They may not have a stable connection, a recent phone, or a data plan that survives the month. An app that only works online is an app that fails exactly when someone needs to confirm what they owe and when.

## What I built

A progressive web app, built with Exploda, that tracks obligations, appointments, and check-ins in one place.

**Offline-first.** The app works with no connection and reconciles when one returns. That is the central architectural decision and it constrains everything downstream: what can be written locally, how conflicts resolve, and what the user sees when the two disagree.

**PWA rather than native.** Installable from a link, no app store, no install friction for someone on a borrowed or low-end device.
